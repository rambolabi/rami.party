What the Doctor checks for

  • Unbalanced braces — every { needs a matching }.
  • Broken environments — every \begin{...} needs a matching \end{...} with the same name, in the right order.
  • Math mode mishaps — unclosed $...$, $$...$$ or \[...\].
  • Missing document scaffolding — no \documentclass or no \begin{document} / \end{document}.
  • Unescaped special characters — a lone %, &, #, _ or $ that LaTeX will choke on outside a table or math mode.
  • Missing packages — commands like \includegraphics or \textcolor used without their \usepackage.
  • Typos in common commands — near-miss spellings such as \beign or \emph{ left unclosed.
