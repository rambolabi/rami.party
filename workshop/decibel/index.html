What the numbers mean

  • 0–30 dB Rustling leaves, a quiet bedroom at night.
  • 30–50 dB Library, soft conversation, a humming fridge.
  • 50–70 dB Normal talking, an office, background TV.
  • 70–85 dB Busy street, vacuum cleaner, a lively restaurant.
  • 85–100 dB Hearing damage after prolonged exposure — power tools, heavy traffic, a loud bar.
  • 100 dB+ Concerts, chainsaws, sirens. Minutes are enough to hurt.

Clamor is an approximate meter built on a browser microphone — it is not a calibrated instrument and should never be used for legal, medical or workplace-safety decisions.
