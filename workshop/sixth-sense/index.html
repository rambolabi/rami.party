Sixth Sense

Your device is covered in senses you don't have. This page wakes every one it can find — accelerometers, magnetometers, light meters, microphones, radios — and turns their whispers into something you can see. Nothing leaves the device.

Some senses need a tap and a permission prompt — that's the browser protecting you. Best on a phone, over HTTPS.
