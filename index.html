How this playground works

Everything here is a small, self-contained website. Here's what you'll find and how to find it.

  • 🗺️

    Three realms

    Projects are sorted by how finished they are: the Gallery of Wonders holds the polished ones, the Workshop holds everything still being built, and the Wastelands keeps retired experiments online instead of deleting them.

  • 🔍

    One search for everything

    The search box below looks through every realm at once — names, tags and a plain-language description of what each project actually does. So “subnet”, “email headers” or “noise meter” find the right tool even if you don't know its whimsical name. Press Esc to clear.

  • 🏷️

    Badges & markers

    Search results are labelled so you know what you're walking into, and cards tell you where a link leads.

    • ✅ Finished ready to use
    • 🚧 WIP works, but still changing
    • ☄️ Archived retired, kept as a relic
    • 🕓 Planned only an idea so far
    • ↗ opens in a new tab
    • 🔒 nothing to visit yet
  • 🕯️

    No accounts, no tracking

    Nothing here asks who you are. The projects are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that run entirely in your browser — anything you type or measure stays on your device. A few tools ask permission for your microphone or Bluetooth; that's the browser asking, and you can always say no.

  • 🧭

    Getting around

    Every realm has a “Back to the playground” pill in its header, so you can always find your way home. The site works on phones, keyboards and screen readers, and it calms its animations down if your system asks for reduced motion.

  • 🌱

    It keeps growing

    New experiments appear in the Workshop first, graduate to the Gallery when they're done, and drift into the Wastelands when they've had their day. Things move — that's the point.
