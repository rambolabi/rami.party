How this place works

New here? Everything you need to know, in under a minute.

  1. 1

    Pick a realm — or just search

    Every project lives in one of three realms below. If you already know what you're after, type it in the search box: it looks through every project in all three realms at once, including the ones hidden deeper in the Workshop.

  2. 2

    Open a card

    Each card is one project. Click anywhere on it to open it. Cards marked ↗ live on another domain and open in a new tab; cards marked 🔒 aren't ready yet, so they don't go anywhere.

  3. 3

    Play — nothing is asked of you

    No account, no cookie banner, no payment. Almost everything runs entirely in your browser, and the projects that remember things (notes, lists, settings) save them on your own device, not on a server.

🏛️ The Gallery of Wonders

Finished projects. Polished, working and safe to recommend to a friend. Start here if you want something that just works.

⚗️ The Workshop

Works in progress. Usable, but still being built — rough edges are part of the deal. This is the biggest realm by far, so it has its own page and its own search.

☄️ The Wastelands

Retired experiments. Old versions and abandoned ideas, kept as they were. Some are broken on purpose — that's the point.

What the labels mean

  • ✅ Finished Done and looked after.
  • 🚧 WIP Works, but still changing.
  • ☄️ Archived Retired to the Wastelands.
  • 🕓 Planned An idea — there's nothing to open yet.

These labels appear on search results. The small pills at the bottom of a card (tool offline) are just keywords — they're searchable too.
